# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Academic institution library staff (System Admins, Librarians) and Patrons (Students, Faculty).

## Product Purpose
Lumina LMS modernizes institutional library management by delivering real-time inventory management, cataloging, circulation, member tracking, fine calculation, and actionable analytics.

## Positioning
An audit-compliant, highly reliable library management system integrating automated policy enforcement, structured penalty governance, and real-time operational insights.

## Operating Context
Campus academic libraries, checkout desks, administrative governance, circulation kiosks, and patron discovery portals. Operates with Supabase backend and Next.js frontend.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Book cataloging, ISBN lookup, physical inventory tracking, and QR code generation/scanning.
- Circulation workflows: borrow, return, renew, reservation handling.
- Fine and penalty calculation, manual overrides, account suspensions.
- Role-based access control (Admin, Librarian, Member).
- Real-time audit logging and system activity tracking.
- Web-based application with responsive dashboard and kiosk support.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Lumina LMS
- Tone: Professional, structured, trustworthy, and efficient.
- Palette foundation: Deep slate navy primary with crisp neutral backgrounds and status semantics.

## Evidence on Hand
- Next.js 16 App Router codebase with Tailwind CSS, Radix UI primitives, Supabase SSR.
- Database audit specifications, client policy questionnaires in `CLIENT_QUESTIONS.md`.

## Product Principles
1. Integrity First: Every circulation change, penalty waiver, and permission elevation is tracked and auditable.
2. Frictionless Circulation: Fast, clear flows for scanning, issuing, and returning books.
3. Clarity and Scannability: High information density balanced with clear visual hierarchy for busy desk workers.
4. Robust Policy Enforcement: Automated rules for due dates, loan limits, and suspensions with sensible administrative overrides.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards with high-contrast text ratios, clear focus indicators, keyboard-navigable dialogs and menus, and accessible data tables.
